# Data sources

Every vehicle data source TRIPY ingests (and serves to yeda-rechev through the facts API). Generated by `scripts/data_sources.py` from the committed manifests; do not edit by hand.

data.gov.il terms permit copying, storage, processing, derived works, commercial use and public display, with the source metadata kept and no misleading presentation or privacy harm. TRIPY stores government datasets at model / model-year level only: no per-vehicle record is stored or served.
